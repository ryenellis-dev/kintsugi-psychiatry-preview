Insurance information

Coverage, made simpler

Many insurance plans cover Spravato. Approval usually takes a little paperwork first, and we help with it.

1

We check your benefits

Send your insurance details with your inquiry, or bring your card to your evaluation.

2

Prior authorization

Most plans ask your prescriber to show Spravato is medically needed. We send this for you.

3

You know your costs

We tell you what your plan covers before treatment starts.

Two bills, two parts

Spravato coverage often has two parts: the medicine itself, and the visit with monitoring. Your plan may cover them differently, and we'll explain both.

Confirm how you bill: do you buy and bill the medicine yourselves, or does a specialty pharmacy ship it to you?

Help with costs

Savings programs

If you have commercial insurance, the maker of Spravato offers a savings program that can lower what you pay for the medicine. People with Medicare, Medicaid or other government plans usually can't use it.

Learn more and check if you qualify at spravato.com, or ask our team. We're happy to help you sign up.
