Services

Focused care for hard-to-treat depression

Our whole practice is built around Spravato treatment, from your first evaluation to ongoing visits.

Core service

Spravato treatment program

You take Spravato nasal spray in our treatment room, then relax for about two hours while our clinical team checks on you. Visits start twice a week, then space out over time.

What a visit looks like
First step

Evaluation for Spravato

A full psychiatric evaluation to review your history, past medications and health, and to decide together whether Spravato is a good fit.
